Stockholm University Computer Science Association

We are a collective of students and doctorates at Stockholm University with a shared interest in computer science (CS) & mathematics. We created Datavetenskapsföreningen as an open forum for networking, discussion and the organisation of CS related activities. We are just getting started, take a look around! about us

Datavetenskapsföreningen was founded in October 2026, our members range widely in backgrounds from cybersecurity to linguistics, economy & more. At the forefront most of us are united by Linux, open-sourcing, programming, philosophy, artificial intelligence & all manner of media which combine these topics.

Any and all interests related to or blossoming from technology and the climate surrounding it are more than welcome :) We hope to hear what brought you here specifically! Stop by at one of our upcoming events or write us @ datavetenskapsforeningen@proton.me
