Stockholm University Computer Science Association

Welcome to our page!

We are a collective of students and doctorates at Stockholm University with a shared interest in computer science (CS), technology and mathematics. We believe that technological literacy is of utmost importance in the spirit of our time. We hope to foster increased understanding and engagement within the field of computer science by creating networking opportunities, organising and participating in related events and through shared contribution to CS projects.

More about us

Datavetenskapsföreningen was founded in October 2026, at the time of writing our members range in backgrounds from cybersecurity and linguistics to economy, philosophy and more. We hope that the association will mature into an open forum for a wide variety of different interests, at the forefront most of us are united by shared interests in Linux, open-source, programming, philosophy, artificial intelligence and all manner of media which combine these topics.

Any and all interests related to or blossoming from technology and the climate surrounding it are welcome, we hope to hear what brought you here specifically :)!
