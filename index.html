What is this?

Stockholm University Computer Science Association

Datavetenskapsföreningen was created in October 2026 as an open forum for networking, discussion & the organisation of computer science related activities at Stockholm University for & by students.

We are just getting started, take a look around! Is this an association for you?

About Us

Datavetenskapsföreningen was founded in October 2026, our members range widely in backgrounds from cybersecurity to linguistics, philosophy & more. Most of us are united by Linux, OSS, programming & all manner of media which combines these topics.

Any and all interests related to technology & the current discourse around it are more than welcome :) We hope to hear what brought you here specifically, stop by at one of our upcoming events or write us!
